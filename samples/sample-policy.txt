Sample Personal Insurance Policy

Cancellation and cooling-off period

The policyholder may cancel this policy within 30 calendar days after the policy start date. If no claim has been made, the insurer will refund the premium paid. The insurer may deduct any non-refundable administrative fee disclosed in the policy schedule.

Cancellation after the cooling-off period

After the first 30 calendar days, the policyholder may cancel by giving written notice. The refund, if any, will be calculated according to the unused portion of the coverage period and the terms shown in the policy schedule.

Claims notification

The policyholder must notify the insurer as soon as reasonably possible after an insured event. Supporting documents should be provided within 60 calendar days unless the insurer agrees to an extension.

This sample is fictional and is provided only for demonstrating the technical assessment service.
